Questions

Common questions

How do I get a quote?

Use the form above or message us on WhatsApp. Tell us what you need, the size, and send a photo if you have one.

Can I bring my own design?

Yes. A sketch, a phone photo or a picture you like is enough to start.

Who can join the training?

Beginners and working carpenters. Ask us which course suits you.

Can I choose the wood and finish?

Tell us your budget and the look you want, and we will advise on the timber and finish.

Where is the workshop?

At Tippa Garage, Bakoteh. Call or message before you come so we can meet you.

Do you repair old furniture?

Yes. We mend, sand and refinish furniture, doors and frames.
